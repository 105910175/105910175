### 🎮 Featured Projects

#### 🛡️ Zero Day: Last Line
**Unreal Engine 5.8 | C++ | Blueprints**

A top-down city defence game developed for the Qualys Games Programming challenge at Swinburne.

Built around the concept of **Detect → Validate → Remediate**, players must identify incoming threats, determine which are dangerous and protect the city.

**My contributions:**
- Player character development and gameplay mechanics.
- Player movement and interactions.
- Audio system and sound effects.
- Player animations.
- Environment and level design.
- Blueprints and gameplay implementation.
- Game demonstration and presentation.

#### 🥉 Self-Stabilising Assistive Utensil
**SSE x Dassault Systèmes Hackathon 2026**

<kbd>3rd Place Achievement</kbd>

Collaborated with engineering students to design a modular, self-stabilising utensil handle for people experiencing hand tremors and mobility limitations.

**Technologies:**
- ESP32 Microcontroller
- MPU-6050 IMU Sensor
- Servo Motors
- 3D Printing
- Embedded Systems

The prototype was designed to support interchangeable spoons, forks and knives in a portable device.
